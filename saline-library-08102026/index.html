Bekka Wilkerson August 10, 2026 11 min read Leave a comment

See all the upcoming library events below or visit the main events page at www.mysaline.com/events

Below are the in-person and virtual programs taking place at the Saline County Library for this week. There is something for all ages, and different categories of activities, like fitness, games, language, crafts, stories, clubs, and of course a New Year celebration this time! Read more below:

Who-Dun-It Book Club

Monday, August 10: 5:00 PM – 5:30 PM

 

Join East End’s whodunnit book club for a fun, interactive four-week journey! Each month, the club kicks off with an intro and chapter plan for this month’s book, and then we dive deeper each week with discussions, theories, and your best guesses at solving the crime. In the final week, we’ll reveal WHO-DUN-IT, celebrate winners with prizes, and unveil next month’s mystery.

 

Learn More

Morning Makers

Tuesday, August 11: 10:30 AM – 11:30 AM

 

Kick off your morning with creativity! Join us each week for a fun, guided craft—perfect for all skill levels. Every session features a new project, and all supplies are provided. Just show up, relax, and get crafty!

This program is for ages 18+.  Registration is required.

 

Learn More

Line Dancing

Tuesday, August 11: 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM

 

Put on your dancing shoes and join us for Line Dancing at the Benton Library! This fun, energetic class is open to adults ages 18 and up. All skill levels are welcome—whether you’re a beginner or a seasoned dancer, you’ll learn easy-to-follow steps and enjoy great music in a relaxed, supportive environment.

 

Learn More

Learn More

Literary Tea Society – Book Club

Wednesday, August 12: 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM

 

The Literary Tea Society is a book club for adults that spans all genres and topics – much like the blends in our tea cabinet. We’re eager for new book recommendations to sample, and welcome new members to the Society. Every month we’ll meet for a lovely cup of tea, with tea blends themed to our current book selection! Join our Society on the 2nd Wednesday of each month from 5:00 PM to 6:30 PM for discussion, sips, and good literary fun!

 

This month: “West with Giraffes” by Lynda Rutledge.

 

 

Learn More

Thursday, August 13: 9:30 AM – 2:00 PM

Walk-ins are welcome; appointments are recommended. Email joelb@salinecountylibrary.orgfor questions or to set up an appointment.

CPR Basics Workshop

Saturday, August 15: 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM

 

Learn how to recognize heart attacks, cardiac arrest and the basics of CPR.  You could help save a life! This informative workshop will introduce participants to the fundamentals of cardiopulmonary resuscitation, including when and how to perform CPR in an emergency situation.

We are pleased to present this workshop in conjunction with Saline Memorial Hospital, whose healthcare professionals will guide participants through essential life-saving techniques and answer questions. This session is designed for beginners and anyone interested in learning practical emergency response skills.

All are welcome. No prior experience is required.
